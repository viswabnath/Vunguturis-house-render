## 3 Design Render Directions

1. **Concept 01 — Front Stairs (Active & 3D Ready)**:
   - Complete 11-stage sequential construction 3D model.
   - Independent external stair flight with landings & walkways for multi-unit rental independence.
   - Ground floor tandem 2-car parking (4.05m × 10.31m) + 1BHK unit.
   - First and Second floors featuring spacious 2BHKs with dedicated Northeast Puja sanctuaries and Southeast Agni kitchens.

2. **Concept 02 — Rear / Back Stairs (Design in Progress)**:
   - Relocates stair circulation to the eastern rear setback/utility area.
   - Frees up the entire West frontage for an uninterrupted modern glass facade and expansive balconies.

3. **Concept 03 — Luxury Duplex Villa (Concept Draft)**:
   - Single-family luxury villa transformation.
   - Dramatic double-height living room cutout connecting First and Second floors with an internal floating staircase.
